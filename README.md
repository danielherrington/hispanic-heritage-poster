# 🌎 Kids Country Poster Board Builder & Learning Kit
### *Designed for Kindergarten & Elementary School Culture Displays (Standard 28″ × 22″ Boards)*

An interactive, kid-friendly web tool that helps children and parents research, customize, and print high-quality school culture display projects—**learning while doing** every step of the way!

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 🎓 Learn While Doing: Interactive Discovery Studio
- **Guided Kid Explorer Questions:** Fun prompts to discuss as a family (cheers to shout out loud, foods to imagine tasting, and history to discover).
- **Hero & Superstar Picker:** Children choose who inspires them most from sports, music, art, and science (e.g. Bad Bunny, Roberto Clemente, Sonia Sotomayor, Frida Kahlo, Dr. Ellen Ochoa, David Ortiz, Shakira, Celia Cruz, and more).
- **Fun Facts Selector:** Kids browse 5–6 fascinating trivia tidbits and select their top 3 favorites to generate custom colorable mini-vignettes.
- **1-Minute Speech Practice & Audio Playback:** Generates a custom kindergarten speaking script and uses browser text-to-speech so the child can listen, practice, and feel confident in class.
- **Pocket Presentation Cue Cards:** Printable cutout flashcards the student can hold in their hand during presentation day.

### 2. 🖨️ Precision Print-Calibrated Sheets (8.5″ × 11″)
- **Sheet 1: Giant Colorable Banner (Rotated 90°):** Prints vertically across the full 11″ paper dimension to produce a giant **9.8″ Wide × 4.8″ High** cutout with hollow bubble letters ready for markers and crayons.
- **Sheets 2–5: Uniform 7.7″ × 4.8″ Cards:** Each features a **5.2″ wide landscape coloring illustration** + **2.1″ wide fact column** covering all 9 teacher rubric categories.
- **Sheet 6: Card 9 (3 Fun Facts) + Cue Cards:** 3 custom colorable drawings plus speaking cards.
- **100% Black & White Line Art:** Zero ink-wasting colors, pure white interiors for coloring, and no parent meta-notes inside the cutout lines.

### 3. 🖼️ Real-Time 28″ × 22″ Poster Board Mockup
- Live interactive preview of how the final tri-fold / flat board will look before printing.
- Includes teacher rubric checklist verification badges (Neat & Colorful ✔, Pictures & Drawings ✔, Labels Included ✔).

### 4. 💾 Save & Load Projects
- One-click JSON export/import to save your child's progress, collaborate, or share with classmates.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

You can launch the tool directly in your browser:

```bash
open index.html
```

Or from the saved artifact link:
```bash
open "/Users/dherrington/.gemini/antigravity/brain/eff36887-82bf-4a2d-9314-901cc3e6c1cb/poster_kit_builder.html"
```

To print:
1. Click the blue **"🖨️ Print 8.5″×11″ Sheets"** button (or press `Cmd + P`).
2. Set Margins to **"None"** or **"Default"**.
3. Turn on **"Background graphics"**.
4. Print all sheets and enjoy coloring together!
